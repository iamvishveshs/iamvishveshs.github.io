Project Overview

Griham simplifies your search for accommodation and essential services. From accommodation listings to food, Gym and laundry, everything is a call away — designed for students and professionals.

Key System Features

Effortless Room Finder

Verified flats, PGs, and shared homes. Smart filters for location, budget, and room type with one-click call buttons for instant contact.

Roommate Matching

Advanced profile filtering by gender, lifestyle traits, and personal habits (smoking, drinking, pets, and dietary preferences).

Advanced Search & Filters

Dynamic query sorting to filter properties based on specific criteria like Type (PG, Hostel, Flat), Status, and custom price ranges.

Admin Control Panel

Dedicated back-office panel allowing staff to approve listings, manage active platform users, and review platform engagement.

Secure Email Integration

Automated transactional messages including registration confirmations and secure password resets driven by PHPMailer via Google SMTP with app passwords.
